The HPE ProLiant DL345 Gen12 is a scalable 2U, single-socket server delivering high compute performance and extensive storage capacity at 1P economics. Optimized for virtualization, software-defined storage, and data management, it supports 5th Gen AMD EPYC processors (up to 192 cores), up to 6 TB memory, PCIe Gen5 I/O, and EDSFF storage. Configure up to 12 LFF, 24 SFF, or 36 EDSFF drives and install up to four front-mounted double-width GPUs for demanding, data-intensive workloads.
