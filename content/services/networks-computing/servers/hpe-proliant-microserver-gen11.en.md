The HPE ProLiant MicroServer Gen11 is an affordable, compact, and powerful entry-level server customizable for on-premises, edge, hybrid cloud, or datacenter workloads. Its ultra mini tower design allows flexible placement—flat, vertical, or wall-mounted. Powered by the latest Intel® Xeon® 6300-series, Xeon® E-2400, or Pentium® processors, it delivers strong performance, security, and remote management with HPE iLO silicon root of trust. With 4 DDR5 DIMM slots and 2 PCIe slots, it's ideal as a general-purpose server, NAS, or for virtualization—offering surprising capability in a small form factor.
