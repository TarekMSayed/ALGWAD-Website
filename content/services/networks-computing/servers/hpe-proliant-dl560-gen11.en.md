Looking for a dense but highly scalable server for your data center application and virtualization needs? Looking for a quad socket enterprise server that is highly scalable for your data intensive workloads in your data center?

The HPE ProLiant DL560 Gen11 server is a high-density, four-socket (4S) server with high performance, scalability, and reliability, all in a 2U chassis. Powered by 4th Gen Intel® Xeon® Scalable Processors with up to 60 cores, the HPE ProLiant DL560 Gen11 server offers greater processing power, up to 16 TB of faster DDR5 memory, I/O of up to six PCIe 5.0 slots, and up to 2 OCP slots.
