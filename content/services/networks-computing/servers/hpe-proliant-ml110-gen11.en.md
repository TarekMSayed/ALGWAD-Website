The HPE ProLiant ML110 Gen11 supports 4th and 5th Gen Intel® Xeon® Scalable processors, up to 1536 GB HPE DDR5 SmartMemory, four x16 PCIe Gen5 slots, one x16 OCP3 slot, and up to eight LFF or 16 SFF drives. It can be rack-mounted, fitting into 5.5U of space. Optional redundant fans and power supplies support automatic fail-over, making it an ideal choice for small to mid-sized businesses as well as remote and branch offices.
