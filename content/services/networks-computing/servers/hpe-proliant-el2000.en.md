The HPE ProLiant Compute EL2000 Chassis is a rugged, 2U modular edge platform designed for industrial environments where space, power, and reliability are critical. Supporting HPE ProLiant Compute EL220 and EL240 servers, EL2000 enables manufacturers, utilities, transportation operators, and energy providers to deploy scalable compute directly at the point of operation. EL220 delivers efficient performance for embedded and cabinet‐based deployments, while EL240 supports higher I/O, expansion, and data‐intensive workloads such as analytics, automation, vision processing, and real‐time control. Together, the platform provides consistent security, simplified management, and dependable operation in harsh and distributed industrial settings.

## Technical specifications

| Spec          | Value |
|---            |---|
| Top workloads | Edge, AI, Data processing, Automation, Video analytics |
| Form factor   | 2U chassis supports up to two (2) HPE Compute EL220 (1U) or one (1) EL240 (2U) servers |
| Processor     | Intel® Xeon® 6, 128 cores (EL220), 144 cores (EL240) |
| GPU           | 2 single-wide / 1 double-wide GPU (EL240 only) |
| Max memory    | Up to 2TB DDR5 |
| Storage       | 2 M.2 NVMe + 4 EDSFF |
| Management    | HPE iLO 7 |
