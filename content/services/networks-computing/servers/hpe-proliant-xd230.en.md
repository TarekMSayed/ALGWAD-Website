Looking for a flexible scale-out solution for your HPC workloads? HPE ProLiant Compute XD230 is an advanced high-performance computing platform designed to deliver exceptional performance and flexibility in conventional HPC applications. Offered in a compact 1U form factor, it supports the latest Intel® Xeon® 6 processors and features a scalable architecture optimized for high-density HPC workloads. Significantly improved bandwidth drives performance while HPE iLO Management ensures reliable security. The platform also incorporates advanced framework options such as HPE Slingshot, along with a cluster manager through HPE Performance Cluster Manager (HPCM), bringing supercomputing capabilities to smaller scales. It also offers cooling options with support for both air-cooled (CLC) configurations and direct liquid cooling.
