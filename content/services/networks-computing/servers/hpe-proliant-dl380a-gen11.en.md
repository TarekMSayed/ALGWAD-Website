The HPE ProLiant DL380a Gen11 is a rack-optimized 2U 2P platform designed for high-performance AI with advanced GPU acceleration. It features three independent power domains—including two dedicated GPU domains with six power supplies each—to deliver efficient, reliable power to both the system and GPUs. Powered by 4th and 5th Gen Intel® Xeon® Scalable processors with up to 56 cores, the HPE ProLiant DL380a Gen11 supports up to four double-wide GPUs and high-throughput PCIe Gen5 I/O. Inference performance can be further enhanced by connecting two adjacent GPUs using NVIDIA's 2-way NVLink Bridge, making it a strong foundation for scalable, secure AI workloads.
