The HPE ProLiant DL345 Gen11 is a scalable 2U, single‑socket server that delivers high compute density and large-capacity storage at efficient 1P economics. It supports 4th- and 5th‑Generation AMD EPYC™ processors with up to 160 cores, up to 3 TB DDR5 memory at 6400 MT/s, and high‑speed PCIe Gen5 I/O. Flexible storage options include EDSFF and up to 20 LFF / 34 SFF / 36 EDSFF drives. With space for up to four front GPUs, it's ideal for data‑intensive workloads. Built‑in HPE silicon root of trust ties firmware to the AMD Secure Processor for validated boot security. Excellent for software‑defined storage, analytics, and virtualization.
