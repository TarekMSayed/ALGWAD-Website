Need a dual-socket server that scales for data analytics and hybrid cloud workloads? The HPE ProLiant DL380 Gen11 is a versatile 2U 2P platform built to handle demanding applications with strong compute performance, high memory density, and fast data movement. Powered by 4th and 5th Gen Intel® Xeon® Scalable processors with up to 64 cores, up to 8 TB of memory, 20 EDSFF drives, and PCIe Gen5 I/O, it's ideal for software-defined storage, video transcoding, and virtualized environments. Engineered for modern IT, the DL380 Gen11 delivers a cloud operating experience, built-in security, and optimized performance to keep your business moving forward.
