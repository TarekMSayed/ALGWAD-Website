The HPE ProLiant ML30 is a powerful, affordable tower server for small, remote, or branch offices, supporting on-premises and hybrid cloud solutions. It delivers enterprise-class performance, security, reliability, and expandability at a low cost, with HPE iLO Silicon Root of Trust built in. Flexible options include redundant power, up to 4 LFF or 8 SFF hot-plug HDDs, and PCIe Gen5 for easy upgrades with SAS controllers or network cards as your business grows.

## Technical specifications

| Spec          | Value |
|---            |---|
| Top workloads | SMB, Remote Office, File/Web/Email Server, Point of Sale, entry AI |
| Form factor   | MicroTower |
| Processor     | Intel® Xeon® E processors up to 8 cores |
| GPU           | Up to one single-wide GPU |
| Max memory    | Up to 128GB DDR5 |
| Storage       | 4 LFF or 8 SFF, HPE NS204i-u Gen11 NVMe Hot Plug |
