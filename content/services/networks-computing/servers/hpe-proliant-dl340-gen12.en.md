The HPE ProLiant Compute DL340 Gen12 delivers strong performance with Intel® Xeon® 6 processors offering up to 144 cores, up to 4 TB of memory, and PCIe Gen5. This 2U, single‑socket server provides excellent datacenter efficiency and supports three CTO chassis options for SFF, LFF, and GPU configurations. Its modular 3‑box front design increases flexibility, while improved thermals reduce power consumption. With its configurability and efficiency, the DL340 Gen12 is ideal for IaaS, PaaS, and SaaS workloads.
