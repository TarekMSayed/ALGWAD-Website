Meet the HPE ProLiant Compute DL380 Gen12—a powerhouse 2U, dual-socket server built to deliver blazing-fast compute performance, massive memory scalability, and lightning-quick data transfer rates. Fueled by the latest Intel® Xeon® 6 processors with up to 144 cores, support for up to a staggering 8 TB of memory, and room for 36 EDSFF E3.S drives, this server is engineered to handle heavy enterprise applications with ease. Designed to future-proof your IT infrastructure, the HPE ProLiant Compute DL380 Gen12 combines advanced security features, performance-optimized architecture, and intelligent, AI-driven automation—making it the ultimate hybrid cloud platform for businesses that demand more from their servers.
