The HPE ProLiant DL20 server offers a compact, versatile, and secure solution at an affordable price. Its short-depth rack form factor is ideal for small, remote, or branch offices, as well as point-of-sale platforms in transport, retail, and hospitality, or for military and government installations where space is limited. With reliable performance and manageability, this one-socket, 1U server powered by Intel® Xeon® E processors delivers enterprise-class capabilities and exceptional value. Flexible configurations and a wide range of options meet diverse business needs.
