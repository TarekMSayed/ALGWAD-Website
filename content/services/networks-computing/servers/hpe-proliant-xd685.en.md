The HPE ProLiant Compute XD685 is powered by a choice of NVIDIA or AMD Instinct™ GPUs, and AMD EPYC 9005 Series CPUs, making it ideal for large-scale AI model training, natural language processing, and multimodal training.

## Technical specifications

| Spec          | Value |
|---            |---|
| Top workloads | Large AI model training and fine-tuning |
| Form factor   | 5U Direct-Liquid Cooled, 6U Air |
| Cooling       | Air or direct-liquid cooling |
| Processor     | 2P 5th Gen AMD EPYC™ Processors |
| GPU           | 8x NVIDIA H200 SXM or 8x NVIDIA Blackwell (B200) |
| Max memory    | Up to 6TB DDR5 |
| Storage       | EDSFF drives |
| Management    | HPE iLO 6, HPE Performance Cluster Manager |
