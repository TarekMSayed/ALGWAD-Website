The HPE ProLiant Compute DL320 Gen12 is a 1U single‑socket rackmount server designed to maximize rack utilization while reducing virtualization risks in power‑constrained environments. It offers greater expansion capabilities than previous generations and delivers strong performance with Intel® Xeon® 6 processors featuring up to 144 cores, expanded memory up to 4 TB, and high‑speed PCIe Gen5. The DL320 Gen12 provides an efficient, high‑performance solution for modern datacenter needs.
