Are you ready for the future 6G and AI at the extreme?

Customized for edge applications requiring high bandwidth and low latency, such as vRAN and future 6G, the HPE ProLiant Compute DL110 server is based on an open, standards-compliant infrastructure and delivers dense I/O capabilities, with PCIe Gen5 speed, acceleration and powerful computing powered by Intel® Xeon® 6 SoC processors. HPE ProLiant Compute DL110 takes up less space thanks to a compact, shallow 1U/1P front chassis and is reinforced to NEBS Level 3 compliance for harsh environments. Thanks to HPE ProLiant's reliable experience with integrated HPE Integrated Lights-Out (iLO) management and security using HPE's silicon trust root, the HPE ProLiant Compute DL110 is ideal for 5G deployments at the communications service provider edge.
