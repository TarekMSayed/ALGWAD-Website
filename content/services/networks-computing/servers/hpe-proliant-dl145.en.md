Power your business everywhere with compute built for the edge. The HPE ProLiant DL145, powered by AMD EPYC™ 8005 processors, supports critical applications, virtualization, and AI workloads. Designed for harsh edge environments, it runs reliably from -5℃ to 55℃ and includes built‑in air filtration for dusty spaces and vibration tolerance. With strong performance, a new ruggedized option kit, simplified management, and enterprise‑grade security, the DL145 delivers dependable computing for demanding industries.
