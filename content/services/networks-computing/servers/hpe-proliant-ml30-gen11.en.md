The HPE ProLiant ML30 Gen11 is a powerful, affordable tower server for small, remote, or branch offices, supporting on-premises and hybrid cloud solutions. It delivers enterprise-class performance, security, reliability, and expandability at a low cost, with HPE iLO Silicon Root of Trust built in. Flexible options include redundant power, up to 4 LFF or 8 SFF hot-plug HDDs, and PCIe Gen5 for easy upgrades with SAS controllers or network cards as your business grows.
