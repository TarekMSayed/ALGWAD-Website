The HPE ProLiant ML350 Gen11 is a high-performance 2-socket tower server that can convert to a rack chassis to suit varied deployments, offering strong compute, security, reliability, and expandability. Equipped with 4th and 5th Gen Intel® Xeon® Scalable processors (up to 64 cores), up to 8 TB DDR5 memory, PCIe Gen5, enhanced I/O and GPU support, plus EDSFF storage, the ML350 Gen11 handles a broad range of demanding workloads. An HPE-exclusive ASIC provides a Silicon Root of Trust, binding server firmware to a processor-specific fingerprint that must match before the server will boot, enhancing firmware integrity. Ideal for IT infrastructure, data management, VDI, ERP/CRM and other diverse workloads, the ML350 Gen11 scales to meet changing needs and helps accelerate business growth.
