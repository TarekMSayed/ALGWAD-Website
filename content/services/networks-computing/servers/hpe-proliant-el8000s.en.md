Compact. Resistant. Prepared for AI.

HPE ProLiant EL8000s is a high-performance blade server designed for environments where space is limited and reliability is critical. With its ultra-dense, shallow chassis, it offers powerful computing capabilities in a compact form factor, ideal for mobile, tactical operations and industrial-end use case deployments. Designed to operate in extreme conditions, the EL8000s supports a wide operating temperature range of 0 to 55°C, ensuring consistent performance in the field. Whether you're running real-time analytics, AI inference, or workloads for crucial tasks, this server is ready to take on the challenge.
