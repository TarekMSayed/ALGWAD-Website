Looking for a space-saving desktop for home or office use? The Dell Slim Desktop is powered by an Intel® Core™ Ultra 5 225 processor with 10 cores, running Windows 11 Home. It features Intel® UHD Graphics, 16GB of DDR5 memory, and a 1TB PCIe SSD for fast, reliable storage — all in a compact form factor.

| Storage         | 1 TB SSD, PCIe |
