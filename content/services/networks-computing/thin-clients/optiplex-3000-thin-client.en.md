Looking for a reliable, fiber-capable thin client for virtual desktop environments? The OptiPlex 3000 Thin Client is powered by an Intel® Celeron® N5105 processor (4 MB cache, 4 cores, 4 threads), running Dell ThinOS 10. It features 16 GB of DDR4 memory (2 x 8 GB) and 64 GB of on-board eMMC storage, delivering efficient performance for thin client deployments.
