Looking for a compact rack-mounted workstation for professional environments? The New Dell Pro Precision 7 R1 Rack is powered by an Intel® Core™ Ultra 5 235 processor (24 MB cache, 14 cores, 14 threads, 2.9 GHz to 5.0 GHz, 65W), running Windows 11 Pro. It features Intel Integrated Graphics, 8 GB of DDR5 memory (1 x 8 GB, 5600 MT/s, UDIMM, Non-ECC), and a 512 GB Gen 5 Performance SSD that's SED ready, all housed in the PWR1260 chassis.
