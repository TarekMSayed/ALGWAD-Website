Need a powerful workstation for professional-grade compute and graphics workloads? The Precision 7960 Tower Workstation is powered by an Intel® Xeon® w5-3425 processor with 12 cores, running Windows 11 Pro for Workstations. It features an NVIDIA® RTX™ A400 graphics card, 32GB of DDR5 memory, and a 512GB Performance SSD that's SED ready, delivering reliable, secure performance for demanding applications.
