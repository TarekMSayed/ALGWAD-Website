Looking for a professional laptop built for mobile workstation tasks? The Dell Pro Precision 5 Series 16S Laptop is powered by a Series 3 Intel® Core™ Ultra 5 336H vPro® processor with 12 cores, running Windows 11 Pro. It comes equipped with Intel® Arc™ Pro Graphics, 16 GB of LPDDR5X memory, and 256 GB of SSD storage, paired with a 16" Touch WUXGA (1920x1200) display.
