Looking for a powerful gaming laptop built for performance? The Alienware 15 Gaming Laptop is powered by an Intel® Core™ 7-240H Series 2 processor with 10 cores, running Windows 11 Home. It comes equipped with NVIDIA® GeForce RTX™ 5050 graphics, 16 GB of DDR5 memory, and 512 GB of SSD storage, paired with a 15.3" WUXGA (1920x1200) display.
