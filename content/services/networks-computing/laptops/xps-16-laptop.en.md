Looking for a powerful laptop with dedicated graphics performance? The XPS 16 Laptop is powered by an Intel® Core™ Ultra 7 255H processor with 16 cores, running Windows 11 Home. It comes equipped with NVIDIA® GeForce RTX™ 5050 graphics, 32 GB of LPDDR5X memory, and 1 TB of SSD storage, paired with a 16.3" Non-Touch 2K (1920x1200) display.
