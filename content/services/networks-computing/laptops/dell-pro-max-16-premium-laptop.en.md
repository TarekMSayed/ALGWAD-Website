Looking for a high-performance business laptop built for demanding workloads? The Dell Pro Max 16 Premium Laptop is powered by an Intel® Core™ Ultra 7 265 vPro® Enterprise processor with 16 cores, running Windows 11 Pro. It comes equipped with NVIDIA® PRO 2000 Blackwell Generation graphics, 32 GB of LPDDR5X memory, and 1 TB Performance SSD storage (SED Ready, TLC), paired with a 16" Non-Touch FHD+ (1920x1200) display.
