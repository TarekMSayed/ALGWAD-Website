Looking for a flexible 2-in-1 laptop for work and everyday use? The Dell 14 Plus 2-in-1 Laptop is powered by an AMD Ryzen™ AI 5 340 processor with 6 cores, running Windows 11 Home. It comes equipped with AMD Radeon™ Graphics, 16 GB of LPDDR5X memory, and 512 GB of SSD storage, paired with a 14" Touch 2K (1920x1200) display.

| Memory           | 16 GB LPDDR5X |
| Storage          | 512 GB SSD |
| Display          | 14" Touch 2K (1920x1200) |
