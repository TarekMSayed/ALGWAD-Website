Looking for a powerful yet portable laptop for everyday productivity and AI-enhanced tasks? The New XPS 13 Laptop is built around Series 3 Intel® Core™ Processors with dedicated AI PC capabilities, running on Windows 11 Home or Windows 11 Pro. It features a stunning 13.4” 2.5K touch screen display, up to 17 hours of battery life, and an ultra-slim, lightweight chassis starting at just 2.2 lbs (1 kg) and 0.5 in (12.7 mm) thin.
