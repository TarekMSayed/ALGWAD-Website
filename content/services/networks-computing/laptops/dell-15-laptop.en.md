Looking for a reliable everyday laptop? The Dell 15 Laptop is powered by an Intel® Core™ 3-100U processor with 6 cores, running Windows 11 Home. It comes equipped with Intel® UHD Graphics, 8 GB of DDR5 memory, and 512 GB of SSD storage, paired with a 15.6" Non-Touch FHD (1920x1080) display in a sleek Carbon Black case.
