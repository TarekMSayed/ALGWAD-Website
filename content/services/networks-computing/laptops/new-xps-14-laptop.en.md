Looking for a capable everyday laptop with a larger display? The New XPS 14 Laptop is powered by a Series 3 Intel® Core™ Ultra 5 325 processor with 8 cores, running Windows 11 Home. It comes equipped with Intel® Graphics, 16 GB of LPDDR5X memory, and 512 GB of SSD storage, paired with a 14" Non-Touch 2K (1920x1200) display.

## Technical specifications

| Spec             | Value |
|---               |---|
| Processor        | Series 3 Intel® Core™ Ultra 5 325, 8 cores |
| Operating System | Windows 11 Home |
| Graphics Card    | Intel® Graphics |
| Memory           | 16 GB LPDDR5X |
| Storage          | 512 GB SSD |
| Display          | 14" Non-Touch 2K (1920x1200) |
